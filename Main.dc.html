THE INITIATIVE

Empowering Safe Technology Navigation

About Fortify

Fortify is an AI-assisted scam detection tool designed to help older adults, caregivers, and community organizations identify suspicious digital communication.

Digital scams targeting seniors have grown increasingly sophisticated. Fortify eliminates technical friction by evaluating standard phone screenshots, providing plain-language risk evaluations without requiring users to copy malicious links or navigate complex settings.

About ElderConnect

ElderConnect is an initiative focused on helping older adults navigate technology safely and confidently.

By bridging human-centered design, digital literacy support, and applied cybersecurity research, ElderConnect develops accessible tools and educational resources that empower seniors to maintain independence and safety in an interconnected world.

STEP 01 — THE THREAT

Scam attempts act on urgency.

Fraudulent messages impersonate family or authority figures, creating artificial pressure to bypass critical thinking.

STEP 02 — THE ACTION

Zero technical setup. Just a screenshot.

No copying links, retyping messages, or adjusting system settings. Taking a simple phone screenshot triggers the evaluation process.

STEP 03 — THE ANALYSIS

Pattern matching AI evaluation.

Fortify analyzes structural text patterns, language pressure tactics, and known fraud indicators.

STEP 04 — THE RESULT

Informed decision support.

Receive a scam risk estimate along with clear guidance on identified risk indicators to help you make informed choices.
