Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fortify by ElderConnect ("we," "us," or "our"), an academic research initiative supported by St. Cloud State University, collects, uses, and protects information gathered through our website and waitlist interest form.

1. Information We Collect

Because our platform is currently in a research and waitlist phase, we only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us through our web forms:

  • Contact Information: First name, last name, and email address.
  • Category / Role Selection: Your indicated interest group (e.g., "For Myself," "For a Family Member," or "For an Organization").
  • Technical & Analytics Data: Basic technical data provided by standard web hosting services (such as IP address, browser type, and device type) strictly for site performance and security monitoring.

Note on AI Features & Screenshots: The interactive tools currently displayed on this landing page are static or client-side educational demonstrations. We do not store, process, or collect personal phone screenshots or private text message logs through this website at this time.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information gathered through our interest form solely for legitimate research and communication purposes:

  • Sending updates regarding project developments, academic milestones, and testing opportunities.
  • Organizing and managing our pilot program interest list.
  • Analyzing high-level demographic interest to guide research funding and program outreach (reported only in aggregated, de-identified metrics).

3. Data Protection & Privacy Guarantee

No Commercial Sale: We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease your personal information to third parties or marketing firms.

Service Providers: Your form submissions are processed securely through our infrastructure provider (Netlify) strictly for form management.

Institutional Integrity: Any reporting provided to institutional supporters or academic bodies relies strictly on aggregated statistics and never includes personal contact details.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill the research and outreach goals of the project. You have the right to request access to your data, request corrections, or request complete deletion from our interest list by contacting us at projectelderconnect@gmail.com.
